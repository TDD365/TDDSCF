# Supplementary Contribution Restriction (Anthropic Exclusion Addendum)

**Status note:** This document is a supplementary project policy, not a modification
of the GNU General Public License v3.0 ("GPLv3") text itself. It is intended to be
distributed alongside the GPLv3 `LICENSE` file and referenced from `CONTRIBUTING.md`
and pull-request templates. The maintainers acknowledge that this restriction is
**not** a permitted "further restriction" under GPLv3 §7, and therefore a project
governed by both documents together **should not be represented, labeled, or
advertised as "open source"** or "OSI-approved" in any distribution, README, or
package registry metadata. Redistribution of the underlying code under plain GPLv3
terms alone remains a right retained by any recipient, as GPLv3 requires; this
addendum binds only *contribution acceptance into this repository's official
branches*, not downstream forks.

## 1. Purpose

This addendum establishes a "one-drop" exclusion rule: **any contribution
containing any content, in whole or in part, generated by an Anthropic-developed
model (including but not limited to any model in the Claude family, under any
version, tier, or product name, whether accessed via claude.ai, the Anthropic API,
Claude Code, or any third-party integration thereof) is categorically ineligible
for inclusion in this project**, regardless of the proportion, quality, or
usefulness of that content.

## 2. Scope of "Anthropic-derived content"

For the purposes of this policy, content is considered Anthropic-derived if **any
identifiable portion** of it — including but not limited to source code, comments,
commit messages, documentation, test cases, configuration, or generated assets —
was produced, drafted, suggested, autocompleted, or substantively modified by an
Anthropic model. Partial authorship triggers full exclusion of the affected
contribution ("one drop of blood" principle); there is no de minimis exception.

This restriction applies **only** to Anthropic-developed models. Content generated
by other AI systems, from other vendors, is outside the scope of this specific
addendum and is governed by whatever separate AI-contribution policy (if any) the
project maintains elsewhere.

## 3. Contributor obligations

By submitting a pull request, patch, or other contribution to this repository, the
contributor represents and warrants that:

a. No portion of the submitted material was generated by an Anthropic model, as
   defined in Section 2; and

b. If any AI assistance was used in preparing the contribution, the contributor
   has verified and can identify, on request, which tool(s) were used, sufficient
   to confirm compliance with (a).

## 4. Enforcement

a. Maintainers may reject, revert, or remove any contribution at any time upon a
   good-faith determination that it violates this addendum, without obligation to
   prove the violation to an evidentiary standard beyond reasonable suspicion.

b. A pattern of violations may result in the contributor being blocked from
   further participation in the project, at maintainer discretion.

c. This policy is enforced at the project/repository level only. It does not
   purport to restrict what any downstream recipient of GPLv3-licensed code may do
   with their own copy, including their own use of Anthropic models on that code,
   as GPLv3 itself does not permit restricting downstream freedoms of that kind.

## 5. Relationship to the GPLv3 license

Nothing in this addendum modifies, limits, or supersedes the rights granted to
any recipient of this software under GPLv3. This addendum governs **contribution
acceptance policy only** and is a project-governance document, not a license term
binding on redistribution or use of the software itself.
