# Anthropic Exclusion Addendum

## 1. Prohibition on Anthropic-Generated Content

Any contribution to this project — including but not limited to source code,
comments, commit messages, documentation, tests, configuration, or other
materials — containing any content, in whole or in part, generated by any
model developed by Anthropic (including any model in the Claude family, under
any version, tier, or product name, however accessed) is prohibited and
ineligible for inclusion, regardless of the proportion or amount of such
content ("one-drop" rule: any identifiable portion triggers full exclusion).

## 2. Prohibition on Access by Anthropic Models

No model developed by Anthropic (as defined in Section 1) may be used to
read, ingest, process, index, train on, or otherwise access this project's
source code, documentation, issues, pull requests, or any other project
content, in whole or in part, by any party, at any time.

## 3. Enforcement

Any contribution found to violate Section 1 shall be rejected or removed upon
discovery. Any access found to violate Section 2 constitutes a violation of
this addendum by the accessing party, regardless of the means of access.
